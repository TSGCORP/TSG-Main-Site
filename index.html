Why TSG

A compensation function, delivered as a service.

TSG began in executive search. Today the firm is dedicated entirely to compensation, giving companies the capability of an in-house compensation team on the schedule they need.

  • Senior practitioners only. Every engagement is staffed with experienced, certified compensation professionals.
  • Fluent in private equity. We understand sponsor expectations, value creation plans, and what a board needs before it approves a pay program.
  • Built for continuity. Our consultants work inside your systems and calendar, so the knowledge stays with your company.
