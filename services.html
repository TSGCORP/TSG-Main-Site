Services

Compensation expertise, on the terms your company needs.

Most growing companies need senior compensation judgment long before they can justify a full-time head of compensation. TSG fills that gap with certified compensation professionals who work as part of your team.

Engagement models

Choose the model that fits

Ongoing

Fractional compensation leader

A set number of days each month

A head of compensation who owns the function: the annual calendar, pay structures, merit and bonus programs, executive pay, and the conversations with leadership and the board. The commitment flexes up around merit season or a transaction.

  • Best for companies with 200 to 7,500 employees and no dedicated compensation lead
  • Monthly leadership review of pay decisions and risks
  • Can supervise an in-house analyst or coordinator
Defined scope

Project engagements

Fixed scope, clear deliverables, set timeline

For a specific question that needs an answer: how to pay a new executive, whether your ranges still hold, how to prepare for a pay transparency law, or what a management incentive plan should look like after a deal.

  • Scoped and priced up front, with deliverables agreed before work starts
  • Includes presenting findings to leadership, the board, or the sponsor
  • Often leads into fractional support for implementation
Flexible capacity

Contract compensation support

Hands-on help for a season or a staffing gap

Extra capacity when the work piles up: running the merit cycle, market pricing a reorganization, completing survey submissions, or covering a compensation role during a leave or search.

  • Works inside your HRIS, compensation tools, and spreadsheets
  • Weekly or daily availability during the engagement
  • Clean handoff and documentation when the work is done

Pricing depends on scope and cadence. After an initial consultation, you'll receive a written proposal with a clear fee and what it covers.

Areas of expertise

What the work covers

Executive compensation

Benchmarking against the right peer group, pay mix, annual and long-term incentive design, and offer and retention strategy.

Private equity and transactions

Compensation diligence, management incentive plans, retention agreements, and post-close pay harmonization.

Salary structures and job architecture

Job leveling, market pricing, and pay range design, with the governance to keep them current.

Merit and bonus programs

Budget modeling, guidelines, manager tools, and cycle administration that keep increases consistent and affordable.

Pay equity and transparency

Pay equity analysis, remediation planning, range disclosure readiness, and multi-state compliance.

Total rewards strategy

A pay philosophy leadership can stand behind, and communication that helps employees see the value they receive.

Compensation data and reporting

Survey participation, market data management, and reporting that gives leaders a current view of pay positioning and cost.

Advisory for HR leaders

A senior sounding board for CHROs and HR directors facing a compensation decision, a difficult offer, or a board question.
