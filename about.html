About TSG

Compensation expertise, built for companies on the move.

TSG is an independent compensation consulting firm serving private equity sponsors, their portfolio companies, and high-growth organizations. We provide the senior compensation capability most growing companies need before they can justify building it in-house.

Our story

From executive search to compensation

TSG was founded in Scottsdale, Arizona, in 2021, with roots in executive search. Placing senior leaders meant sitting inside the offers, counteroffers, and retention conversations that decide whether a hire succeeds, and it showed us how often those decisions were made without the right compensation information.

That insight reshaped the firm. Today TSG is dedicated entirely to compensation: executive pay, salary structures, incentive design, pay equity, and the steady guidance HR leaders need between projects, delivered by fractional and contract compensation professionals.
